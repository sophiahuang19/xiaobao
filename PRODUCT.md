# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Chinese-speaking visitors taking a quiet break on their phone or desktop, spending a moment with Xiaobao, a beloved seal bicolor ragdoll cat.

## Product Purpose
A small companion experience: pet Xiaobao, explore his moods, and draw a comforting fortune to save or share. Keep the existing lightweight static website and its sixty fortunes.

## Brand Personality
Warm, gentle, playful. Xiaobao feels like a familiar pet with a personality and responds to attention.

## Anti-references
Avoid crowded dashboards, harsh full-screen transitions, excessive hearts, and interfaces that hide the main actions.

## Design Principles
- Give the cat room to be the focus.
- Make every interaction produce clear, gentle feedback.
- Keep drawing a fortune simple and approachable.
- Preserve the cat's recognizable face in generated imagery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible actions, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and scrollable small-screen layouts.
